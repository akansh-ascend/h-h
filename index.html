Web Platform

The clinical and operational system of record — used by physicians, nurses, care coordinators, fleet dispatchers, and admins to run the entire home-hospital program end to end: intake, care delivery, monitoring, scheduling, and administration.

A proposed companion mobile app for the people delivering care — admins, physicians, nurses, technicians, and coordinators — sitting alongside the existing patient app. Access to every module would be admin-assignable per role or per person, the same way Roles & Permissions already works on the web platform today.
